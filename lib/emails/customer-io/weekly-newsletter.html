Why bone density drops in months 3–6 on a GLP-1

Rapid weight loss puts your skeleton through a measurable stress response. In the first few months on a GLP-1, your body is shedding weight faster than your bones can adapt — and bone mineral density follows the curve.

The good news: it’s monitorable, and it’s reversible if you catch it early. A baseline DEXA scan in months 1–2, repeated at month 6, gives you a real picture of what’s changing under the scale.
