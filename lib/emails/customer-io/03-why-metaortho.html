The story

Why MetaOrtho exists.

A few years ago, as GLP-1 prescribing exploded, something quieter was happening underneath it:

Patients were losing weight — and also losing strength. And bone density. And often, not knowing it.

The data caught up in 2026. Orthopedic research published through the AAOS pointed to meaningfully elevated osteoporosis risk, gout incidence, and lean-mass loss in long-term GLP-1 users — the kind of changes that don’t show up on a scale, and don’t show up in a six-month visit with your prescriber.

That’s the gap we built MetaOrtho to close. Not to compete with your GLP-1 program. To run alongside it. You keep your prescriber. We handle the part they don’t have the time or tools for.
